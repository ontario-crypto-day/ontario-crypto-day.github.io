**Ontario Cryptography Day** is a recurring event that aims to bring together the cryptography community in Ontario for a day of research talks and discussions.

The event is open to academic, industry, and public sector researchers and practitioners in cryptography, but registration is required.

## Travel

**Location:** [Mike & Ophelia Lazaridis Quantum-Nano Centre (QNC)](https://maps.app.goo.gl/3P1JhtvBLKHhwLQR9), 200 University Avenue West, Waterloo, Ontario, room QNC 0101.

**Parking:** The closest visitor parking lots to the Quantum-Nano Centre are [Lot M](https://maps.app.goo.gl/pa3bmnyLBW3S3Nd67), [Lot N](https://maps.app.goo.gl/Ew65JkcPTafPj79S6), and [Lot Q](https://maps.app.goo.gl/KCtv98mu2kJUTwYa9), which cost about $10–$12/day and must be paid using the [AMP Mobile pay app](https://uwaterloo.ca/sustainable-transportation/visitors). See the University of Waterloo's [visitor parking information](https://uwaterloo.ca/sustainable-transportation/visitors) for more details.


**Public transport:**

- [GO Transit](https://www.gotransit.com/en): Some GO Transit buses come directly to campus, and GO trains to Kitchener station connect with GRT buses/light rail from Kitchener to the university. Check the trip planner on the GO Transit website for more details.
- [Grand River Transit](https://www.grt.ca/en/): The ION light rail stops at the [University of Waterloo station](https://maps.app.goo.gl/pAdNsNZxX9tB7FN17), a short walk from QNC. There are also many local buses that reach the University of Waterloo campus.
